SoundLab DJ privacy policy

Effective 19 November 2026
The short version: SoundLab DJ has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your music, recordings and progress stay on your own device. We don't collect, sell or share personal information.

What stays on your device

The app keeps these in your browser's or device's own storage, and they are not sent to us:

  • Music files you add, and their playlists, tempo, key and cue points
  • Mixes you record, vocals you sample and scratch clips you make
  • Lesson stars, badges, your optional DJ name, and settings

You can delete all of it at any time by clearing the app's or site's data in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Microphone

The app only uses the microphone when you turn it on (the MIC button or sampling from the mic). The sound is processed on your device. Anything you record stays on your device unless you choose to share it.

Sharing

Clips, progress cards and challenge results are only shared when you tap Share or Save, using your device's own share options. Once shared, the app or person you send them to handles them under their own policies.

Services the app relies on

  • Hosting: the app (including its fonts) is served by GitHub Pages, and it doesn't contact any other service. Like any web server, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address for security and reliability. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Payments (only if an upgrade is offered): if you buy an upgrade, the payment is handled by the payment provider shown at checkout, and we receive the licence key and the email you give them so we can support your purchase.

Children

SoundLab DJ is intended for people aged 13 and over. Younger students may use it with a parent or teacher. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, and the app has no accounts, ads or tracking.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
