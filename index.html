Tips

Tips guide you through your first mix step by step: play a track, sync the second deck, swap the bass, crossfade, scratch, set a hot cue, add an effect and record.

A yellow box shows what to press next. Each step moves on once you've done it, or press Skip.

Outputs

Bluetooth speakers play a little late, so your headphones end up ahead of the speaker. Play the test clicks and slide until the speaker and headphone clicks sound like one.

Split cue works on any stereo output, including a phone. Use a 3.5 mm to 2× RCA cable: white goes to the speakers, red to your headphones.

Microphone

The mic goes into the master, so it is heard on the speakers and captured in recordings. Its level knob sits in the mixer under CUE VOL. Keep it away from the speakers to avoid feedback.

MIDI controller

No controllers connected.

MIDI Learn: click a control on screen, then move the matching knob, fader or button on your controller. For a jog wheel, click the platter, spin the wheel, then touch its top. Mappings are saved in this browser.
